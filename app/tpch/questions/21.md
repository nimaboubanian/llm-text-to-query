"We need to audit delivery accountability among our Saudi Arabian suppliers and flag those who have a track record of being the sole cause of delays on otherwise completed orders. For each supplier based in Saudi Arabia, count their late line items on such orders — that is, every line item of theirs that reached the customer after its committed delivery date, on a fully fulfilled multi-supplier order where they were the only supplier who failed to deliver on time — meaning the order itself was fully closed, at least one other supplier participated in the same order and managed to deliver on time, but this particular supplier's items arrived after their committed delivery date. Report the top 100 such suppliers, showing their name and their count of late line items, ranked from the most delays to the fewest, with ties broken alphabetically by supplier name."
